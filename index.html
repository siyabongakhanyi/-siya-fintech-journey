03 / THE SIX-LENS METHOD

How I will analyse a financial product.

The end goal is not to become “a designer who knows fintech”. It is to build a repeatable decision framework that works across customer, system and business realities.

01

Customer Experience

Intent, trust, friction, journeys, failure moments, service recovery and measurable outcomes.

QUESTION → Does the product create clear value for the person using it?
02

Operating Model

Ownership, teams, handoffs, exception handling, policies, controls and service operations.

QUESTION → Who must do what for the promise to work?
03

Technical Architecture

Systems, APIs, events, state, data, integration patterns, systems of record and failure recovery.

QUESTION → How is the capability actually implemented and sustained?
04

Payment Infrastructure

Issuing, acquiring, schemes/rails, authorisation, clearing, settlement and reconciliation.

QUESTION → How does the financial instruction and value move?
05

Risk & Controls

Fraud, AML, data, operational resilience, access, disputes, controls and regulatory obligations.

QUESTION → What can fail, be abused or create unacceptable exposure?
06

Commercial Economics

Pricing, TPV, take rate, cost-to-serve, processing costs, losses, margin and product P&L.

QUESTION → Does the product create sustainable economic value?
